LunarSerpent

Lunar surface analysis & rover traverse planning

Phase 0 · Documentation complete 9 documents 215 KB Math typeset with KaTeX

A scientific decision-support system built on LOLA topography, the Robbins crater catalogue and Chandrayaan-2 imagery. Every displayed number traces to a documented formula with a peer-reviewed source; where a quantity cannot be determined with justified confidence, the system states its uncertainty rather than producing a plausible-looking value.
